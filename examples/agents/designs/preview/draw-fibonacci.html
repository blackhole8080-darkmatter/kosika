Fibonacci Sequence Diagram

A diagram illustrating the Fibonacci sequence and its relation to the golden spiral. Hover a sentence to see it in the drawing.

1Each number is the sum of the two before it: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34.

2Draw a square for each number, every one laid against the edge of the ones before, turning a quarter each time.

3A quarter circle through each square, corner to corner, joins into one smooth spiral: the golden spiral.

4Divide each number by the one before and the answer settles on φ = 1.618: here 34 / 21 = 1.619.

5Sunflowers, pinecones and pineapples grow their seeds and scales one golden angle (137.5°) apart, so their spirals come in neighbouring Fibonacci numbers, like 21 and 34.
