Building on stage 5, give the kernel a screen driver in its own files (drivers/screen.c and
screen.h, drivers/ports.c and ports.h for port_byte_in and port_byte_out). clear_screen()
blanks all 80x25 cells; kprint(char*) prints at the cursor, handles '\n', moves the hardware
cursor through the VGA ports 0x3d4/0x3d5, and scrolls everything up one row when printing
past the last row. main() clears the screen and prints "MayaOS line 1" to "MayaOS line 40",
one a line (write your own small int-to-text function), so after scrolling the screen holds
lines 16 to 40 and the last line printed is on the bottom row.
