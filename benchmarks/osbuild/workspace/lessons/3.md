Expect: Loaded from sector 2
Expect: 0xFACE
---
The boot sector loads the next two sectors of the disk (sectors 2 and 3, using int 0x13
with ah 0x02 and the boot drive the BIOS left in dl) to 0x9000. If the read fails it prints
"Disk read error". os-image.bin is the boot sector followed by those two sectors (pad them
to 512 bytes each). Sector 2 begins with the zero-terminated text "Loaded from sector 2";
sector 3 begins with the word 0xFACE. After loading, the boot sector prints the text at
0x9000 and then, on the next line, the word at 0x9000+512 as hex (0xFACE), then loops.
