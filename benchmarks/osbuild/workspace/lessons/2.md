Building on stage 1: a boot sector with two functions in their own files, included with
%include: print_string (prints a zero-terminated string whose address is in bx) and
print_hex (prints the 16-bit value in dx as 0x followed by four upper-case hex digits).
It sets up a stack, prints "Hello from MayaOS" on one line, then on the next lines prints
0x1F2E and 0xBEEF with print_hex, then loops forever. Functions must save and restore the
registers they use (pusha/popa).
