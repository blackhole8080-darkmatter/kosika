One doctrine, two languages

An audit of Kosika's visual system as it stands, with the numbers computed live by this page. Nothing here restyles Studio; it shows where the doctrine holds and where it is silently broken.

Structure is cyan, intelligence is violet, authority is amber. A colour is never decoration. Glow means “now”. Numbers are monospaced. Diagrams are evidence.
— ui/README.md, the declared doctrine

1 · The two palettes

Left: the doctrine's “lab” tokens from ui/kosika.css. Right: what Studio actually renders — the embedded palette extracted from app_assets/index.html. Both are coherent. Only one is declared.

Lab (declared)

Cool near-black glass, neon signals with named meanings

Studio (current)

Warm earth darks, coral/ember/sage accents, no stated meanings

3 · The bridge: fix the meaning, free the temperature

The doctrine only legislates meaning. The resolution is not to repaint Studio cool — it is to give Studio's accents the same semantic roles, so a model is violet and an approval is amber whether the glass underneath is cool or warm.

4 · Light, disciplined

Three levels exist. Only the third may glow, and only for live state. If everything glows, nothing is live.
